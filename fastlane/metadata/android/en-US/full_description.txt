SpendLens turns the payment notifications and bank messages already on your
phone into a record of your spending, laid out as days rather than as a
dashboard. It reads them where they land and writes what it learns to an
encrypted database on the device.

It cannot send that anywhere. The app holds no INTERNET permission at all, which
is checkable on the APK rather than something you have to take on trust:

    aapt2 dump badging SpendLens.apk | grep uses-permission

<b>What it does</b>

* Reads UPI payment notifications from the apps you already use
* Reads bank SMS, in the "full" build only, including messages already in your
  inbox — usually months or years of history
* Imports a CSV statement exported from your bank
* Lets you type in cash and anything else it cannot see
* Splits a payment, a day, or a run of days between people, and tracks who has
  paid you back
* Tags payments, including trips, which know their own dates
* Shows where the money went, by day, merchant, tag and rail

<b>What it will not do</b>

No account. No sign-up. No analytics, no crash reporting, no advertising SDK, no
third-party services of any kind. Your ledger is encrypted with SQLCipher under a
key held in the Android Keystore, and it never leaves the phone because there is
no code path that could carry it off.

<b>Two builds</b>

The <b>standard</b> build reads notifications only and asks for no dangerous
permissions.

The <b>full</b> build adds SMS reading. This is the only way to recover spending
from before you installed the app: Android exposes no notification history to
third-party apps, so notifications can only ever be read going forward, while the
bank messages already sitting in your inbox go back years. Install this one only
if you want that.
