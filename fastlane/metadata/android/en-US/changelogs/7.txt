Some payment apps later re-announce payments they already reported, such as an
evening "2 new completed transaction updates" notification. SpendLens counted
those twice. They are now ignored, rows already counted that way are flagged as
duplicates, and the same payment reported late by another app is shown but not
counted. Repeat payments, like a second chai, still count. Any row can be
marked as a duplicate, or counted again, from its detail sheet.
